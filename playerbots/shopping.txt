# What a bot buys when it runs short, and where. One need per line:
#   name  item-type  low  buy  place-type...
# item-type is an ItemType name; low is how few is too few (counted in the pack);
# buy is how many it asks for (fewer when its gold does not stretch); the place
# types are destinations.json "Type" values where a vendor of it stands.
# Who needs what: Bandage a bot with Healing, Reagent a caster, WeaponArrow a bow
# user, WeaponBolt a crossbow user, Rune a caster with Mark and Recall (blank runes,
# counted as the blank and worn-out ones it carries, while it has room for more),
# Food a bot that went to eat and found its pack bare (an innkeeper sells it).
# A potion need names the spell in the bottle (Potion:<spell>; a Heal or a Greater
# Heal bottle both count as a heal potion): for a fighter who does not cast.
