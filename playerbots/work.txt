# The trades bots work at, where they ply them and where they sell.
# Skills by their SkillType name, items by their ItemType name, places by the
# destinations.json "Type" values. A bot has a trade when it has the skill at 30.0 or
# more; the tool it lacks is bought (shopping.txt).
#
# gather <skill> <tool type> <place type>...
#   The tool is double-clicked and its target cursor answered on a tile of the skill's
#   resource (rock, a tree, water) near the place.
gather Mining         WeaponMacePick  MiningSpot
gather Lumberjacking  WeaponAxe       LumberSpot
gather Fishing        FishPole        Dock
#
# smelt <place type>...
#   Where ore is carried to and double-clicked beside the forge.
smelt Forge
#
# craft <skill> <tool type> hand|pack <material type,...> <place type>...
#   hand: the tool must be held (a smith's hammer); pack: it is carried.
#   The materials are what the craft's recipes may be made of; a bot keeps them
#   (never sells them) and buys them when it gathers nothing itself (shopping.txt).
#   Smithing needs a forge and cooking a fire within reach (a forge is one).
craft Blacksmithing  WeaponMaceSmith  hand  Ingot              Forge
craft Carpentry      Carpentry        pack  Log,Board          VendorCarpenter
craft Tailoring      SewingKit        pack  Cloth,ClothBolt    VendorTailor
craft Cooking        CookingTool      pack  Fish,MeatRaw,FoodRaw  Forge
#
# sell <item type | prefix* | *> <place type>...
#   Where goods of a type are taken; the vendor there that offers the most gets them.
#   prefix* is every item type whose name starts so; * is everything else.
sell Ingot      VendorSmith VendorProvisioner VendorWeaponer
sell Ore        VendorSmith
sell Log        VendorCarpenter VendorBowyer VendorProvisioner
sell Board      VendorCarpenter VendorProvisioner
sell Fish       Dock VendorProvisioner
sell Hide       VendorTailor
sell Leather    VendorTailor
sell Clothing   VendorTailor VendorProvisioner
sell Cloth      VendorTailor
sell Weapon*    VendorWeaponer VendorSmith VendorProvisioner
sell Armor*     VendorSmith VendorWeaponer VendorProvisioner
sell Shield     VendorSmith VendorWeaponer VendorProvisioner
sell Container  VendorCarpenter VendorProvisioner
sell Musical    VendorCarpenter
sell Carpentry  VendorCarpenter VendorProvisioner
sell Food       VendorProvisioner
sell *          VendorProvisioner
